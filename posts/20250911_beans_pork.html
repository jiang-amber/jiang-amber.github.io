Black Beans and Rice with Pork and Apples

09/11/2025 - The recipe for beans and rice is by Liz Della Croce; the recipe for pork chops is by Ali Slagle.

Black Beans and Rice

  • 1 pound black beans
  • 2 cups long grain white rice rinsed
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 3 cloves whole garlic
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium onion diced
  • 1 red bell pepper seeded and diced
  • 4 bay leaves, divided
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne
  • minced cilantro and lime wedges to serve

Pork Chops and Apples

  • 3 tablespoons light brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1½ tablespoons white miso
  • 2 bone-in pork chops, patted very dry
  • salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1 large Granny Smith or other tart-crisp apples, cored then sliced ¼ inch thick

Journal

Written 09/09/2025, prior to cooking.

The first and only prior time I've had pork and apples together in one dish, I was a senior at UCSD. I remember cooking in my small apartment kitchen, the only separation between the kitchen island and living-room-turned-bedroom being a yellow curtain on a rod. Although not the most lavish living situation, those memories evoke a feeling of coziness and nostalgia. It was a warm dish I served to myself as I settled in for the night.

I've long lost the original recipe I first used, but this new one provides much of the same taste profile. It's finally cooling down in the Bay Area, and I'm hoping it evokes a similar sense of comfort when I have dinner with my boyfriend on 09/11, who will be landing from a flight from Minnesota to California just in time to eat. He's carefully arranged his work schedule so he'd be able to fly and see me twice in one month, so I think he deserves the princess treatment. Therefore, I'm also making black beans and rice since that dish is a staple in his diet, but I think I can make it better than him (don't tell him that).

Modifications

For the black beans and rice, I used two bell peppers rather than one. For the pork, I used honey instead of brown sugar, and pork chop ends rather than bone-in. Seasonings were eyeballed and adjusted to taste.
